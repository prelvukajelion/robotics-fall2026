Throughout these three missions, I learned that getting a model to give a useful response doesn't always mean it will perform the task correctly. In the first mission, making my request more specific helped the model understand what the robot could actually do without assuming things about the cup. In the second mission, the model kept identifying the tool as a measuring tape, even when I asked it to explain what it saw. This showed me that a more detailed answer isn't always a more accurate one. The third mission was probably the most interesting because we could actually see how the robot responded to instructions. Even with a clear route, it kept trying to move into walls and wasted steps stopping. My improved prompt reduced the blocked moves slightly, but the robot still never reached its goal. Overall, I learned that models still struggle with following instructions consistently, even when given enough information.
